
================================================================================
HYPOTHESIS 3b: IMF SLOPE ACROSS EXERCISE PHASES BY WanT (ANAEROBIC POWER) GROUP
================================================================================

HYPOTHESIS:
Anaerobic power (WanT) predicts the rate of neuromuscular fatigue, such that 
cyclists with higher WanT will show different IMF decline patterns during 
high-intensity exercise.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GROUPING METHOD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WanT Median split at 729.50 W
- High-WanT group (≥ 729.50): n = 30
- Low-WanT group (< 729.50): n = 30

WanT STATISTICS:
Mean = 741.58 W
SD = 171.24
Range = [319.0, 1370.0]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Two-way mixed ANOVA examining IMF slope across exercise phases and between 
WanT groups.

- Within-subjects factor: Phase (5 levels: Warmup, Second Threshold, VO₂max, WanT, Cooldown)
- Between-subjects factor: WanT Group (2 levels: High-WanT, Low-WanT)
- Dependent variable: IMF Slope (Hz/s)

Participants were classified based on their Wingate anaerobic power (WanT). 
A median split was used to create High-WanT (n = 30) and Low-WanT 
(n = 30) groups.

Post-hoc comparisons used Bonferroni correction.
Effect size for phase effect: Kendall's W (from Friedman test) - calculated separately by group
Effect size for ANOVA: Partial eta-squared (η²p)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PHASE EFFECT:
Significant main effects of phase on IMF slope were found for 7/8 muscles.

Kendall's W (Phase Effect Size) BY GROUP:

High-WanT Group (average W = 0.183, small effect):
  • SCML: χ²(4) = 39.17, p = 6.42e-08, W = 0.338 (Medium)
  • SCMR: χ²(4) = 17.60, p = 1.48e-03, W = 0.152 (Small)
  • MTL: χ²(4) = 13.57, p = 8.79e-03, W = 0.117 (Small)
  • MTR: χ²(4) = 14.95, p = 4.80e-03, W = 0.129 (Small)
  • STL: χ²(4) = 32.19, p = 1.75e-06, W = 0.278 (Small)
  • STR: χ²(4) = 20.61, p = 3.79e-04, W = 0.178 (Small)
  • UTL: χ²(4) = 20.41, p = 4.14e-04, W = 0.176 (Small)
  • UTR: χ²(4) = 11.59, p = 2.07e-02, W = 0.100 (Negligible)

Low-WanT Group (average W = 0.188, small effect):
  • SCML: χ²(4) = 29.24, p = 6.98e-06, W = 0.252 (Small)
  • SCMR: χ²(4) = 22.81, p = 1.38e-04, W = 0.197 (Small)
  • MTL: χ²(4) = 26.32, p = 2.73e-05, W = 0.227 (Small)
  • MTR: χ²(4) = 26.23, p = 2.84e-05, W = 0.226 (Small)
  • STL: χ²(4) = 19.97, p = 5.06e-04, W = 0.172 (Small)
  • STR: χ²(4) = 25.13, p = 4.73e-05, W = 0.217 (Small)
  • UTL: χ²(4) = 8.80, p = 6.63e-02, W = 0.076 (Negligible)
  • UTR: χ²(4) = 15.34, p = 4.04e-03, W = 0.137 (Small)


WanT GROUP EFFECT:
Significant main effects of WanT group were found for 0/8 muscles.



PHASE × WanT GROUP INTERACTION:
Significant interaction effects were found for 0/8 muscles.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Kendall's W effect sizes were calculated separately for each WanT group 
to examine whether the magnitude of phase-related changes in IMF slope differs 
between anaerobic power levels.

The Low-WanT group showed similar phase effect sizes 
(average W difference = -0.005), suggesting that both groups showed similar patterns of IMF change across exercise phases.

While significant phase effects were observed, the interaction between phase and WanT group was not significant for most muscles. This suggests that anaerobic power level does not substantially moderate the rate of neuromuscular fatigue (IMF decline) across exercise phases.

The absence of significant group effects suggests that WanT level does not independently predict the rate of IMF decline.

A more negative IMF slope indicates greater fatigue development. 

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATISTICAL DETAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Grouping: Median split on WanT (Wingate anaerobic power)
Test: Two-way Mixed ANOVA (Phase × WanT Group)
Post-hoc: Bonferroni correction
Effect sizes: Kendall's W (phase, calculated separately by group), Partial η² (ANOVA)
Alpha level: 0.05
Software: Python 3.x (pingouin, scipy.stats)
