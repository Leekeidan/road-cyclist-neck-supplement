HYPOTHESIS 1 (FITNESS CLUSTER-BASED): NECK ANGLES ACROSS EXERCISE PHASES
BY FITNESS CLUSTER
================================================================================

GROUPING METHOD:
K-means clustering (k=2) on fitness variables:
- VO₂max (mL/kg/min)
- Peak Power output (Watts)
- Years of cycling experience

Cluster Distribution:
- High-Fitness cluster: n = 360
- Low-Fitness cluster: n = 531

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANGLE PLANES ANALYZED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Sagittal plane  (flexion / extension)
2. Frontal plane   (lateral bending)
3. Horizontal plane (axial rotation)

Directional interpretation:
- Sagittal:   Negative = Extension,             Positive = Flexion
- Frontal:    Negative = Right lateral bending,  Positive = Left lateral bending
- Horizontal: Negative = Right rotation,         Positive = Left rotation

Note: Angle values with |angle| > 90° were excluded as outliers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANALYSIS 1: TWO-WAY MIXED ANOVA (Phase × Fitness Cluster)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Within-subjects factor: Phase (5 levels: Warmup, Second Threshold, VO₂max, WanT, Cooldown)
Between-subjects factor: Fitness Cluster (2 levels: High-Fitness, Low-Fitness)
Dependent variable: Mean neck angle (degrees, averaged across time points within each phase)

PHASE EFFECT:
Significant main effects of phase on mean neck angle were found for
2/3 angle planes.

  - Sagittal: F(4,212) = 16.32, p = 0.0000, η²p = 0.235 *
  - Frontal: F(4,200) = 2.22, p = 0.0686, η²p = 0.042
  - Horizontal: F(4,212) = 2.50, p = 0.0435, η²p = 0.045 *

FITNESS CLUSTER EFFECT:
Significant main effects of Fitness cluster were found for
0/3 angle planes.

  - Sagittal: F(1,53) = 0.34, p = 0.5610, η²p = 0.006
  - Frontal: F(1,50) = 0.72, p = 0.4012, η²p = 0.014

Average Kendall's W = 0.087 (Negligible effect)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

No significant Phase × Fitness Cluster interactions were found for neck angles, suggesting that fitness-based groupings do not differentiate neck positioning across exercise phases.

Mann-Whitney U tests did not reveal significant group differences at individual phases after Bonferroni correction.

ANGLE INTERPRETATION:
- Sagittal angles reflect neck flexion-extension posture during cycling
- Frontal angles reflect lateral head tilt
- Horizontal angles reflect axial head rotation
- Changes across phases may reflect postural adaptations to exercise intensity

COMPARISON WITH CCI ANALYSIS:
- CCI captures neuromuscular coordination (muscle co-contraction patterns)
- Angles capture biomechanical posture (neck positioning)
- Together, these analyses reveal whether fitness level predicts both
  neuromuscular and biomechanical adaptations during graded cycling exercise.
